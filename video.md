## ⚙️ 2. Pre-Recording Checklist

Before you hit record, prepare your browser setup:

1. **Browser**: Open Chrome or Edge in **Full Screen (`F11`)** to hide browser address bar and tabs.
2. **Zoom Level**: Set browser zoom to **100%** (or **110%** if text needs to look bigger on mobile screens).
3. **Demo Data**: Make sure you have sample teams (e.g., MI, CSK, RCB, KKR) and 10–15 sample players with photos loaded in the database.
4. **Sound Effects**: Ensure speaker volume is enabled so auction hammer sounds / sound effects are recorded clearly.
5. **Cursor**: Use a clean mouse cursor (turn off glowing/trailing pointer effects).

## ✂️ 4. Video Assembly & Editing Guide

### Recommended Free Video Editors
1. **CapCut Desktop (Windows)** *(Highly Recommended)*: Easy to add trendy text animations, sound effects, transitions, auto-captions, and speed ramps.
2. **DaVinci Resolve** (Free): Professional color grading and audio mixing.
3. **Clipchamp (Windows)**: Simple drag-and-drop timeline editor.

---

### 📱 Two Formats to Create:

#### Format A: Instagram Reel / YouTube Shorts (Vertical 9:16 - 1080x1920)
* **Target Duration**: 30 – 60 seconds (Fast-paced, energetic)
* **Background Music**: Upbeat phonk / modern electronic / IPL anthem remix.
* **Structure**:
  - `00:00 - 00:05`: **Hook**: "Stop using paper for cricket auctions! Look at this..."
  - `00:05 - 00:20`: Quick montage: Lucky Draw Wheel + Fast Bidding (+10k, +50k) + SOLD Stamp!
  - `00:20 - 00:40`: Live Projector Display + OBS TV-style Overlay on YouTube Live.
  - `00:40 - 00:50`: Real-time Team Purse & Squad Stats.
  - `00:50 - 01:00`: **Call To Action**: "Link in bio / WhatsApp us to host your tournament today!"

#### Format B: YouTube Full Demo Video (Horizontal 16:9 - 1920x1080)
* **Target Duration**: 3 – 5 minutes (Detailed walkthrough)
* **Structure**:
  - `00:00 - 00:30`: Introduction & Overview of Cricket Auction Panel.
  - `00:30 - 01:30`: Setup: Teams, Budgets, Player Registration.
  - `01:30 - 03:00`: Live Bidding Arena & Full-screen Projector demo.
  - `03:00 - 04:00`: OBS YouTube Live Broadcast Overlay & Stats.
  - `04:00 - 04:30`: Pricing, Support, and How to Book.
